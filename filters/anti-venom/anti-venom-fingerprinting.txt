! - Only vendors/libraries whose primary purpose is identifying browsers across sites.
! - Rules are third-party only, so a site that self-hosts a script for its own

!   checks, CAPTCHA providers) are deliberately excluded: blocking them breaks
!   logins and payments. EasyPrivacy covers many additional trackers.
